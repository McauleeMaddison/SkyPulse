### 2. Purpose and audience

SkyPulse is an offline, one-tap arcade flying game for iPhone. The player guides a mechanical bird through gates, earns one score point for each cleared gate, collects crystals, unlocks cosmetic birds, and buys crystal-collection upgrades. The game is intended for casual arcade players who enjoy short, replayable score-chasing sessions. It provides entertainment through a simple skill-based challenge with persistent offline progression across three visual worlds.

### 3. Setup and access instructions

To access the main features:

1. Launch SkyPulse. No setup or credentials are required.
2. Tap **Play** on the main menu.
3. Tap anywhere in the gameplay area to flap and guide the bird through the gates.
4. Use the control at the top left to pause or return to the menu.
5. Open **Bird Hangar** from the main menu to view and unlock cosmetic birds using crystals earned during play.
6. Open **Upgrades** to spend earned crystals on Crystal Resonator and Salvage Codec upgrades.
7. Open **Privacy** from the main menu to view the app's privacy information and public privacy/support links.
