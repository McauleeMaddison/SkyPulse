# SkyPulse 1.0.0 (3) — tester notes

SkyPulse is an offline, one-touch cyberpunk flying game. Tap to flap, clear gates, collect crystals and unlock cosmetic birds and collection upgrades. There is no account, advertising or real-money purchasing.

This candidate introduces a gradual opening difficulty ramp, smoother changes between worlds, cleaned-up feedback effects, distinct power-up fields, safe-area UI fitting and a redesigned icon.

Please test:

- Learn the controls in the first three gates, then assess whether gates 4–15 build challenge fairly.
- Reach the world changes at scores 15, 30 and 45, and assess the added speed increases at scores 40 and 60. Report sudden difficulty spikes, unavoidable gates or confusing collisions.
- Collect crystals and each power-up. Check Aegis, Time Pulse and Crystal Magnet remain legible on each background.
- Unlock/equip birds, buy upgrades with earned crystals, scroll both collections, and retry after a crash.
- Pause, background the app and resume. Confirm progress survives relaunch and offline play.
- Play for 15 minutes and report stutters, overheating, audio problems or missed touches, with device model and iOS version.

Use TestFlight's screenshot feedback to include the screen, score and action that preceded any issue.
