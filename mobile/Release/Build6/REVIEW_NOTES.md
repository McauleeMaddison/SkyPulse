## App Review Notes

SkyPulse Arcade is an offline game. No login or account is required. All birds and upgrades use crystals earned by playing; there are no real-money purchases.

Build 6 supports native portrait layouts on iPhone and iPad, fitted to the available screen and safe area.

Feature walkthrough for build 6:
1. Home immediately shows your owned birds, installed Tech levels and next world milestone.
2. Tap Bird Hangar. Browse the 15 mechanical birds with the previous/next controls or a horizontal swipe. The first bird is available from a fresh save. Other birds are cosmetic unlocks bought with earned crystals; all birds share handling.
3. Tap Tech Tree (also available directly from Home). Explore Collection, Recovery and Mastery: nine nodes with three levels each. Tap a node for its actual effect, price and prerequisites. Level 2 in the preceding node unlocks the next node in that branch. Purchases need earned crystals, but browsing/details do not.
4. From Home, tap Worlds & Power-ups. This guide shows the real route worlds and thresholds: Neon City at launch, Aurora Rise at score 15, Solar Drift at 30. It also shows Aegis, Time Pulse and Crystal Magnet with their effects. These are playable route content and collectible pickups, not separately purchased worlds.
5. Return Home, tap Play, then tap the gameplay area to flap. Each gate awards one point. Collect crystals and temporary power-ups; Aegis absorbs a collision, Time Pulse slows flight for 4 seconds, and Crystal Magnet attracts crystals for 6 seconds. Worlds rotate every 15 gates.
6. After a run, use Retry, Bird Hangar or Tech Tree. Progress persists locally between launches. The top-right gameplay control pauses. Privacy on Home opens the app's notice and public privacy/support links.

Build 6 makes these existing systems easier to discover through a progression summary, consistent Tech Tree naming and a route/pickup guide. The Hangar now shows actual crystal unlock progress instead of differing performance bars, since all birds share handling. It also includes the current mechanical-bird Hangar, connected Tech Tree and updated world artwork from the maintained project.
