## Description
Build your flock. Develop your Tech Tree. Take your next flight further.

SkyPulse Arcade combines one-tap flying with a persistent collection of mechanical birds and crystal-powered upgrades. Every run gives you another chance to earn crystals, pursue a new bird and develop your collection.

YOUR BIRD HANGAR
Discover 15 animated mechanical birds and unlock their cosmetic designs with crystals earned through play. Browse the Hangar, choose your signature bird and take it into the next run. Every bird shares the same handling: your timing makes the difference.

THREE BRANCHES OF TECHNOLOGY
Spend crystals on nine upgrade nodes, each with three levels. Collection improves crystal reach and value. Recovery builds your end-of-run rewards. Mastery rewards precision and flight milestones. Develop prerequisite nodes to open the next step in each branch.

ONE ROUTE THROUGH THREE WORLDS
Start in Neon City, reach Aurora Rise at score 15 and Solar Drift at score 30. Worlds rotate every 15 gates as the endless route develops. Home shows your next world milestone alongside your bird and technology progress.

POWER-UPS WORTH CHASING
Collect Aegis to absorb a collision, Time Pulse to slow flight temporarily, or Crystal Magnet to draw in nearby crystals. Open Worlds & Power-ups on Home to see the route and learn each pickup before a run.

PLAY ON IPHONE AND IPAD
Portrait layouts fit your screen and safe area on iPhone and iPad.

PLAY OFFLINE
No account, advertising or real-money purchases. Crystals are earned by playing. Scores, crystals, birds, upgrade levels and preferences are saved on your device. Reduced Motion and haptic controls let you adjust the presentation.
