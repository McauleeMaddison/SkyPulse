# Build 22 — accepted-device media plan

**Status: preparation only. No fresh store media has been approved or uploaded.**
Capture after visual acceptance on the exact release candidate. If runtime fixes
require Build 23 or later, capture that accepted build and update every record.

## Screenshots

Use natural gameplay and the owner's legitimate progress. Record device model,
OS, version/build, date, screen, resolution and SHA-256 with each final file.
Avoid notifications, touch/debug overlays and unrelated personal data.

| Order | Screen | What must read clearly |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Endless Route, Neon City | Selected 3D bird, wing pose, detailed lit districts and a readable opening |
| 2 | A different naturally reached Endless world | Its own atmosphere and matching pipes; no duplicated bird/obstacle |
| 3 | Pulse Relay | Numbered crystals and gate charge; actual hollow hoop and runway |
| 4 | Blackout / Signal Heist | Named restoration above the board, live route, trace and readable objectives |
| 5 | Bird Hangar | An owned bird on the rotating cradle, clear equip/trait information |
| 6 | Tech Tree | Connected branches, next purchasable level and exact price |
| 7 | Training, breeding or earned mastery | A real available preview/reward; no fabricated unlock or balance |

iPhone 17 Pro Max native portrait capture is 1320 × 2868. For the supported iPad,
obtain current 13-inch captures in an accepted size from a genuine running app.
Apple requires iPhone screenshots and iPad screenshots for apps supporting iPad;
use the current required wells shown in Media Manager. JPEG/PNG must be opaque,
with 1–10 screenshots per device size. Do not stretch a phone image into iPad UI.

## Optional public App Preview

Target a 25–30 second portrait clip: about 8 seconds of Endless, 8 of Relay,
8 of Blackout and a short Hangar finish. Show actual wing motion, crystal unlock,
beam rotation and restoration. Use the app's sound; no unrelated licensed music
or effects that falsely imply functionality. Keep important UI readable.

Apple's current preview limits are 15–30 seconds, up to 500 MB and 30 FPS.
Use an accepted preview resolution for its device well, rather than assuming the
native screenshot resolution is accepted for video. Check codec, audio and poster
frame against the [current specification](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-preview-specifications/).
A preview is optional; current accurate screenshots remain essential.

## Private App Review walkthrough

Record a separate concise walkthrough of all three modes, Home switching,
Hangar selection, Tech/Core, training/breeding and saved progress. Do not force

Attach only after the accepted candidate is known; replace the historical build 7
walkthrough after preserving its original. This is evidence preparation, not an
instruction to contact Apple or submit the app.
