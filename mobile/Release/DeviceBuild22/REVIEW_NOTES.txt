LATEST FEATURES
Twelve detailed 3D worlds now use connected island districts, cliff galleries, supported ledges, themed occupied windows, matching LED pipes and restrained coloured atmosphere. Native mechanical birds retain their tap-driven wing animation and equal flight handling. The Tech Tree has twelve upgrade families and 36 levels. Core evolution, individual bird training, breeding, seven pickups and clear purchase previews support collection.

Blackout's eight named restoration rewards add permanent lighting networks to all twelve Endless worlds. Four optional authored daily heist designs use the existing optical rules and local saved attempts. Bird-specific display-cradle mastery, Home next-goal cards, clear run rewards and interrupted daily extraction Retry Save are included.

WALKTHROUGH
1. Home opens on Endless Route. Use the top-left mode badge to switch modes. Tap the playable area to flap; interactive UI is excluded. Worlds change every 15 gates.
2. Pulse Relay has four circuits per UTC day. Collect two numbered crystals to unlock the matching gate automatically before impact; lasers change red to blue. Missing charge leaves it locked. Clear the circuit and choose Next Mission.
3. Signal Heist: tap mirrors/prisms, route energy to all cores and the uplink, manage trace, and use PLAN for a free exact route/cost preview. Splitters divide beams; powered relays open shutters. Intermediate vaults advance immediately. Blackout has no bird upgrades or Hangar.
4. HOW TO PLAY covers the current worlds, pickups, Core, Tech, training, breeding and puzzle systems. Pause, retry, Main Menu, Reduced Motion, sound and haptic preferences are available.

No player sign-in, advertising or real-money purchases. Progress stays on device.

DEVELOPER AND IMPLEMENTATION
Developed by Mcaulee Maddison under his MacWebStudio brand, macwebstudio.com. The game uses native Unity 3D bird prefabs, modelled island districts and world structures, and themed 3D pipes. Bundle identifier, Apple developer account and signing identity are retained. Development history and asset records are retained internally.

The developer states he has never had another Apple developer account and has not supplied the project through another account, publisher, contractor or purchased game template. Apple's private comparison remains unidentified; this draft does not claim that concern is resolved.
