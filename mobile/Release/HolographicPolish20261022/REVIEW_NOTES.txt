CONTROLS AND WALKTHROUGH
1. Home starts on Endless Route. Tap the top-left badge to choose one of three free offline modes. Each has its own Home and relevant controls. HOW TO PLAY explains mode controls; Endless adds Tech Tree, Core, training, breeding and Worlds & Power-ups. Blackout has no Bird Hangar or Tech Tree.
2. Endless: tap the playable area to flap through pipes and collect crystals. Twelve worlds change every 15 gates and wrap at score 180. Cyan/pink pipes have LED inlays, clean collar joins and a departure fade. Wing strokes, banking and a soft tapered engine trail are presentation only; tested flight handling, width, spacing and collisions are retained.
3. Relay: four circuits refresh each UTC day. Collect two randomly placed numbered crystals for their matching gate. At 2/2 the gate unlocks before same-frame collision, red lasers turn blue and locking hardware retracts. No manual FLAP/PULSE action is required; opening animation never delays passage. Hollow 3D hoops draw their near lip in front of the bird. A result offers Next Mission when another circuit remains.
4. Blackout / Signal Heist: rotate mirrors and splitters to power all cores and the uplink. Relays hold matching shutters open. A turn costs 1 trace, or 3 while a red sensor is lit. PLAN previews the next beam/cost without spending. Each clear saves once and activates the next vault immediately; input fences prevent old touches crossing over. Eight systems provide persistent objectives. Budget exhaustion fails; retry starts fresh. Unfinished configurations retain orientation and trace.
5. Bird training improves Endless reward traits. Each completed training level contributes 2% founder-bird unlock savings, capped at 20%. Core levels 2–5 provide separate 5/10/15/20% discounts on Tech, training and breeding, plus capacity. Core evolution has its own cost. Breeding unlocks at Core 2: preview two owned parents, inherited traits and the exact cost. Both parents stay; one permanent offspring is added and equipped. Duplicate owned combinations are guarded. Bird handling stays equal; traits/Tech do not alter Relay.
6. The connected 3D Tech Tree pans in both directions. Nine families have three explicit Installed/Next/Locked levels. Inspect then confirm purchases. Four daily tasks award five crystals each, up to 20 per UTC day. Main Menu, pause/retry, Reduced Motion, sound and haptics remain available.
