CURRENT WALKTHROUGH
1. Home starts on Endless Route. Its top-left badge opens the three free modes. Each Home has its own animated environment, progress and relevant controls. Blackout has its sector map and field guide, without Bird Hangar or Tech Tree.
2. Endless Route: tap the playable area to flap, pass gates and collect crystals. Twelve environments change every 15 gates. Fifteen birds share flight handling; unlocks are cosmetic. Nine Tech Tree nodes have three levels each. The developer requested slightly wider pipe spacing and slightly smaller gaps for build 11.
3. Pulse Relay: four named, bounded circuits refresh each UTC day, with two to five gates. Tap to flap. Faceted numbered crystals open only their matching gate automatically; the opening animation cannot delay passage. There are no FLAP/PULSE buttons. Today's routes and medals survive restarts. A successful mission offers Next Mission directly when another daily circuit remains.
4. Blackout Protocol: eight increasingly hard authored sectors. Tap connected neighbours, recover every core and reach the uplink. Ice/violet keys and mint/amber phase routes add constraints. Tapping a closed firewall spends one breach charge and three trace; tap again to enter. Wrong moves and failed breaches cost trace. Exhaustion fails the sector. Completion saves once and activates the next sector immediately; held input cannot carry into it. The final sector uses the campaign result flow.
5. Main Menu is available after results in all modes. Four daily tasks award five crystals each, at most 20 per UTC day, with separate claim recovery. Existing Endless saves and the earned-crystal economy remain compatible.

BUILD 12 POLISH
