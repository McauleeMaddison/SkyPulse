Home opens on Endless Route. Tap the top-left mode badge to expand the three free modes; select a mode and then press PLAY. Bird Hangar, Tech Tree, Worlds & Power-ups, privacy and Mission Control remain accessible.

1. ENDLESS ROUTE → PLAY. This dispatches to the retained flight simulation: same tap buffering, flap/gravity, hitboxes, gate sizing, difficulty curves, score rules, twelve environments, crystal values and temporary effects. It remains the only Endless high score.
2. PULSE RELAY → PLAY → FIRST CIRCUIT → START MISSION. Tap the playfield or FLAP to pilot the equipped bird. Collect the gold illuminated charge cells; the charge counter is separate from wallet crystals. The next relay is numbered and has a cyan activation window. While 0.6–6 world units ahead, align within the indicated window and press PULSE to spend one charge. Fly through every activated shutter, then through the green exit. The tutorial and three other circuits are available immediately. Mission results save medals without awarding a wallet bonus. Aegis is collectible in First Circuit and Highwire; charge pickups are not crystal pickups.
3. BLACKOUT PROTOCOL → PLAY → PACKET RECOVERY → START MISSION. Tap adjacent nodes to move, collect both DATA packets, then enter EXIT. Move = +1 trace. Firewall Fork, Quiet Bypass and Dark Weave add firewall choices: ARM HACK and tap an adjacent FIREWALL tile, spending one charge and +3 trace without moving; tap the now-open tile normally to move. All puzzles have verified budget-feasible solutions. Puzzle results save separate medals and do not overwrite the Endless score.
4. HOME → MISSION CONTROL. Four fixed, deterministic UTC-day tasks track actual Endless gate passes, successful relay activations, introductory puzzle completion and flight crystal pickups. Progress carries across attempts. Ready tasks claim exactly five wallet crystals; duplicate claims are blocked, with 20 maximum from daily tasks per period. Claims bypass run collection totals and upgrade/result multipliers. Reset is midnight UTC and displayed in local time; backward clock changes retain the saved period. Offline save recovery is not server-backed protection.
5. Pause each new mode, leave the app, return and press RESUME. Retry and mode switching clear temporary sessions, hazards, charges and input state. Return to Home does not award completion rewards.
