WALKTHROUGH
1. Home starts on Endless Route. The top-left badge opens three free modes. Their Homes have distinct scenery, animation, progress and relevant actions. Blackout has its sector map and field guide, without Bird Hangar or Tech Tree.
2. Endless Route: tap to flap, pass gates and collect crystals. Twelve worlds change every 15 gates. Fifteen birds share handling; unlocks are cosmetic. Nine Tech Tree nodes each have three levels. The build 11 owner-requested spacing/gap tuning is retained.
3. Pulse Relay: four daily finite circuits, with two to five gates, refresh each UTC day. Tap freely to flap. Both randomly placed numbered crystals are required for their matching gate. 0/2 and 1/2 stay locked; 2/2 opens immediately, before same-frame impact checks. Shields cannot bypass missing crystals. No FLAP/PULSE buttons. The day’s random layout and medals survive relaunch. Smaller hollow 3D hoops render far hardware behind the pilot and near lips in front. The visible runway edge matches ground collision. A clear offers Next Mission when another daily circuit remains.
4. Blackout: eight increasingly hard authored sectors. Tap connected neighbours, recover cores and reach the uplink. Ice/violet keys and mint/amber phase routes constrain the puzzle. A closed firewall tap spends one breach charge and three trace; tap again to enter. Wrong moves/failed breaches spend resources; exhaustion fails. Each clear saves once and activates the next board immediately. Held/queued input is fenced; the final sector uses the campaign result flow.
5. Main Menu is available after each mode’s results. Four daily tasks each award five crystals, at most 20 daily-task crystals per UTC day, with claim recovery. Existing save keys and earned-crystal economy remain compatible. Reduced Motion and haptics settings are available. No login, ads, analytics or real-money purchases.
