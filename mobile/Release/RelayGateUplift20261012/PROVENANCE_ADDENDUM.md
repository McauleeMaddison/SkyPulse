The generator original remains in place; its bytes were copied unchanged into Unity Resources with new asset/folder GUIDs. Its use is confined to Pulse Relay gate/exit/Home presentation. No external pack, font, shader, CLI or API-key fallback was used.

Codex implemented the supporting presentation code: a procedural elliptical energy membrane, shutter retraction, rim illumination and hardware reuse in Relay Home/exit. The original Endless runtime file, mission-rule model, daily rules, existing artwork, asset GUIDs, scene, packages and signing configuration are retained. The source comparison and dated ledger distinguish repository history from independently authenticated authorship.

The older owner-declared ARTA artwork and later generated backgrounds retain their separate records. Existing reference/origin/permission gaps and Apple's unidentified comparator remain unresolved; a generation record does not certify exclusivity or global uniqueness. No release or review approval is claimed.
