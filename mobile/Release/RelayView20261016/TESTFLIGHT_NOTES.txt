SkyPulse 1.0.0 (15) — random crystal pairs, smaller 3D hoops and visible ground

Pulse Relay remains free tap-to-flap flight. Both matching numbered crystals are required for each gate: 0/2 and 1/2 stay locked; 2/2 opens immediately. Crystals are randomly placed ahead of their gate. Daily layouts remain stable across retries/relaunches and refresh each UTC day. There is no imposed climb/dive pattern or connecting guide path. Gates vary randomly across more of the playable height; the compact counts/hint/pause panel sits at the real top safe edge. Gate labels and the flight hint show the remaining requirement. Shields cannot bypass a locked gate.

Hoops have genuine hollow 3D mesh hardware: smaller opening, bevelled metal, curved housings, recessed bore, raised collars and moving inset lights. The bird enters the hole, passes behind the near rim and emerges. The runway's bright edge marks the actual ground collision height. Bird handling, screen width, sounds, saves, wallet and other modes remain preserved.
