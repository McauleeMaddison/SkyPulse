# SkyPulse support

Publication draft: public contact confirmed; hosting pending.

SkyPulse is an offline, one-tap flying game for iPhone. Tap Play, then tap the flight area to flap. Use the top-left pause button to pause or return to the menu.

## Progress and crystals

Crystals are earned through play. Bird Hangar and Tech Tree purchases spend earned crystals; this version has no real-money purchases. Birds share the same flight physics.

Scores, crystals, birds, upgrades and preferences are saved on your device. There is no SkyPulse account or cloud-sync service. Deleting the app may remove progress. Do not reinstall the app as a troubleshooting step unless you accept that risk.

## Settings

Reduced Motion limits scenery and effects animation. Haptics can be switched off in the game settings.

## Report a problem

Contact: [macwebstudio26@gmail.com](mailto:macwebstudio26@gmail.com).

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, SkyPulse version, what happened and the steps that reproduce the problem. A screenshot is useful when reporting a visual issue. Do not send passwords or payment information.
