WALKTHROUGH
1. Home opens on Endless Route. Tap the top-left badge for three free offline modes. Each Home has an isolated rendered 3D stage, animations, progress and relevant controls. The old enclosing black Home panel is removed. Blackout has a vault map and field guide, without Bird Hangar or Tech Tree.
2. Endless: tap to flap, pass gates and collect crystals. Twelve worlds change every 15 gates. Fifteen birds share flight handling; unlocks are cosmetic. One connected holographic Tech Tree pans horizontally and vertically, with nine families of three levels. Prices, prerequisites and saves are retained. The owner-requested build 11 spacing/gap tuning is retained. Gate meshes follow the established collider transforms; no flight controls change.
3. Relay: four finite circuits refresh each UTC day, with two to five gates. Tap freely to flap. Two randomly placed numbered crystals are required for their own gate. 0/2 and 1/2 remain locked, even under shields; 2/2 unlocks before same-frame collision. The first crystal charges half the ring. The second switches red lasers blue and retracts locking hardware; passage never waits for artwork. Hollow mesh hoops draw near hardware in front of the bird. The information panel sits at the safe top edge; the visible runway matches ground failure. A clear offers Next Mission when another daily circuit remains.
4. Blackout is redesigned as Signal Heist: eight authored optical vaults, replacing the old tile-walking game. A true 3D optical console uses fixed projected touch targets. Tap glass mirrors/splitters to rotate them. All cores and the uplink must be powered simultaneously. Splitters branch forward/left; relays open matching shutters only while energized. Turns cost 1 trace, or 3 while a red sensor is lit. Budget exhaustion fails. Minimum solution costs rise 2/4/6/7/10/12/14/16. Intermediate extraction saves once and activates the next puzzle immediately; input generations prevent held or queued taps leaking across. The eighth vault has the final result flow. Attempts save prism orientation and trace; retry starts fresh. Prior achievements retain unlocked access without falsely awarding medals for new layouts.
5. Main Menu is available after each mode’s result. Four daily tasks each award five crystals, at most 20 per UTC day, with claim recovery. Existing wallet/collection/Endless saves remain compatible. Reduced Motion, sound and haptics settings are retained. No login, advertising, analytics or real-money purchases; progress stays on device.
