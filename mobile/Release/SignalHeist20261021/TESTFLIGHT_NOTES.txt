SkyPulse Arcade 1.0.0 (17) — Signal Heist

BLACKOUT IS NOW A SIGNAL-ROUTING HEIST
Tap silver mirror prisms and crystal splitters to rotate them. Route the live laser through EVERY core and the uplink at the same time. Power relays A/B to open matching shutters. A rotation costs 1 trace, or 3 while a red sensor is lit. Spend the budget without extracting and retry. Eight authored vaults increase from 2 to 16 minimum turns. Clears activate the next vault immediately. The old tile-walking game is removed.

ALL THREE MODES HAVE DEPTH
Endless adds cylindrical gate meshes, Relay retains its hollow titanium hoops, and Blackout has an angled 3D optical console. Each Home has its own rendered 3D stage; the enclosing black panel is removed. Controls press visually without shrinking their hit rectangles. Illustrated character/world artwork is retained.

PULSE RELAY VISUALS
First crystal charges half the hoop; the second turns red lasers blue and unlocks immediately. Hollow mesh hoops, eight faceted crystal meshes in the four-gate circuit, moving station machinery, reactive engine trails and articulated bird artwork. Original tap-to-flap controls, shared width and Endless handling are retained.

PLEASE TEST ON YOUR PHONE
Rotate rapidly, pause/resume, background/reopen an unfinished vault, exhaust trace and retry, finish vault one and check the next is already playable, then finish all eight. Check Reduced Motion, sound/haptics and readability. In Relay verify 0/2 and 1/2 gates stay locked, 2/2 opens automatically and ground collision remains visible. Check all three mode Homes and Main Menu after results.

Unity automated checks and native delivery status are in the release record. Physical-device performance and feel still need your feedback. Public App Review is on hold; this is private testing only.
