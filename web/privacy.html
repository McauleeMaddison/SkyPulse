PRIVACY

SkyPulse is a friend beta. It does not require an account and does not collect or send personal information.

YOUR GAME DATA

Your score, crests, unlocked cosmetics, settings, and daily-flight progress are stored only in the browser on your own device. Clearing browser data removes that local progress.

FEEDBACK

The optional feedback button opens your device’s sharing sheet with a draft message. You choose whether to send it and who receives it.
